Mecanum Drive

Instantaneous omnidirectional translation & rotation

Holonomic (3 DOF)

Mecanum drive utilizes four specialized wheels whose rims are ringed with passive rollers angled at 45° to the wheel plane. By coordinating wheel spin directions, diagonal forces sum constructively to glide in any vector.

          STUDENT GUIDE
        
      

        
      
          How 45° Mecanum Rollers Work: Because rollers spin freely along their axis, each wheel can only push against the ground perpendicular to the roller (at 45°). By pairing forward and reverse motor spins, components cancel or add together:
        
      

        
      
          
      
            1. Forward Drive
            FL(+) FR(+) RL(+) RR(+)
            
      Lateral forces (+Fx vs -Fx) cancel to 0. Forward forces add: +4·Fy.
      
          
      
          
      
            2. Pure Strafe Right
            FL(+) FR(-) RL(-) RR(+)
            
      Forward forces (+Fy vs -Fy) cancel to 0. Lateral forces sum rightward: +4·Fx.
      
          
      
          
      
            3. Rotate In-Place
            FL(+) FR(-) RL(+) RR(-)
            
      All translational forces cancel to 0. Generates pure angular torque: τ = 4·F·R.
